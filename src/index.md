## Core Thesis: Cultivating Health, Not Just Chasing Disease

Rather than treating chronic illness solely as isolated organ failures, the CDR framework reframes many complex, treatment-resistant conditions as cells **trapped inside a protective defense loop** that failed to receive the systemic safety signals needed to finish healing and rejoin the [Health Cycle](concepts/health-cycle-six-elements.md).

```text
  └────────▲─────────┘                                            │

  ┌────────┴─────────┐                                            │
  │  CDR3: Re-entry  │ <──────────────────────────────────────────┘
  │  Differentiate & │
  │  reintegrate     │    (Failure to exit ──> chronic illness / hypometabolic stall)
  └──────────────────┘
```
