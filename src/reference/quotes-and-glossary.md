# Key Quotes & Glossary

## Attributed Key Quotes

### On the Six Cardinal Elements & Functional Reserve
> "The six cardinal elements of the health cycle are 1) wakeful activity, 2) nutrient intake, 3) waste and toxin removal, 4) social connection [and positive social support], 5) nature connection, and 6) restorative sleep. Absence of, or abnormalities in, any of the cardinal elements of the health cycle can lead to pluricausal disease [while] their presence contributes to the building of functional reserve capacity and resilient health over the weeks and months after injury or illness."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

### On Cultivating Health vs. Chasing Diseases
> "Medicine typically focuses on identifying and 'treating' diseases rather than identifying what creates health and cultivating that. Although the disease-focused model can often seem very impressive (and generate a lot of business from each identified 'disease'), without cultivating the core generators of health, complex chronic diseases will continually arise that cannot be fixed within the disease-centered model."
> — **A Midwestern Doctor**

### On Safety Signals & Healing
> "If long-distance safety signals are not re-established after the initial damage from infection or injury are repaired, the tissue defaults to chronic defense at the cellular level."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

### On Induction Therapy vs. Lifelong Medication
> "Antipurinergic drugs like suramin, pannexin 1 (PANX1) channel blockers, and other salugenesis therapies are part of induction therapy to reboot healing. They are not meant to be taken for life. These interventions are meant to be bridges. During the induction phase, interventions are meant to act like a cast for a broken leg—they are intended to support the healing process and protect injured tissues from further damage. Once the healing cycle is completed and reentry into the health cycle occurs, then antipurinergic therapy can be discontinued."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

### On Long-COVID & ME/CFS as Hypometabolic States
> "Evidence is emerging that patients with long-COVID, also known as post-acute sequelae of SARS-CoV-2 infection (PASC), do not have a generalized defect in mitochondria. Instead of a generalized defect, long-COVID is associated with entry into a specifically altered state of [hypometabolic] mitochondria function... Restoration of long-distance brain-body signal transduction at the end of Phase 3 of the Healing Cycle [reduces purinergic signaling]... and enables normal organ function to return, and marks the re-entry to Health Cycle."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in This Framework |
| --- | --- |
| **Antipurinergic Drugs (APDs)** | Compounds that block purinergic ATP danger receptors (e.g., suramin, Brilliant Blue G, P2X7 inhibitors) to quiet chronic CDR loops |
| **Cell Danger Response (CDR)** | Conserved mitochondrial defense shift that prioritizes cell survival over normal organ work and signals nearby tissues to brace for threat |
| **CDR1** | Phase 1 (inflammatory): cells seal off and neutralize microbial/toxic threats; chronic lock-in drives inflammatory conditions |
| **CDR2** | Phase 2 (proliferative): replaces biomass, vessels, and stem cells; chronic lock-in drives proliferative disorders including cancer |
| **CDR3** | Phase 3 (reintegration & differentiation): inflammation resolves and cells reconnect with nerves/hormones; chronic lock-in drives neurodevelopmental, psychiatric, and neurodegenerative conditions |
| **Functional Reserve Capacity** | Whole-organism resilience built over weeks/months when the Six Cardinal Elements of the Health Cycle are consistently present |
| **Health Cycle** | Normal energetic, communicative, restorative daily physiology governed by Naviaux's six cardinal elements |
| **Healing Cycle** | Naviaux's updated name for the full CDR1 → CDR2 → CDR3 repair arc when it successfully completes and exits |
| **Hypometabolic Survival State** | Low-energy cellular hibernation shunt seen in ME/CFS, long-COVID, and chronic Lyme where mitochondria stall between normal work and full repair |
| **Induction Therapy** | Temporary "cast for a broken leg" interventions used only until the Healing Cycle completes and the body re-enters the Health Cycle |
| **Integrated Stress Response (ISR)** | Conserved protein-synthesis-dampening pathway activated by misfolded proteins/stress that overlaps CDR biology in age-related cognitive decline |
| **Mast Cell Activation** | Hypersensitive immune degranulation (histamine release in CDR1) regulated by extracellular purines like ATP, ADP, adenosine, and UDP-glucose |
| **Pluricausal Disease** | Chronic illness arising from cumulative deficits across multiple health generators and combined low-grade stressors rather than one single pathogen or lesion |
| **Pretox** | Gentle pre-conditioning of elimination and circulation pathways prior to active detoxification so hypersensitive CDR patients don't crash |
| **Purinergic Signaling** | Danger-warning network driven by extracellular ATP (~1,000,000× higher inside cells than outside) binding 19 cell-surface purine receptors |
| **Salugenesis** | Active creation of health via safety signals (vagal tone, deep sleep, natural rhythms, and the six cardinal elements) to terminate chronic defense |
| **Suramin** | Century-old sleeping sickness drug and broad-spectrum purinergic receptor blocker tested in Naviaux's low-dose autism pilot and PaxMedica's follow-up RCT |
| **Telogen Effluvium** | Hair thinning from follicles remaining stuck in the resting (`telogen`) phase instead of re-entering active growth (`anagen`) |
| **Zeta Potential** | Electrical repulsion stability keeping suspended particles/cells dispersed in biological fluids; cited by the author as important for microcirculation and toxin drainage |
