Cell Danger Response & The Health Cycle

About & Source Scope

This wiki is an educational study guide distilling the publicly available first half of:

A Midwestern Doctor (Jul 5, 2023). "Systemic Treatments for the Cell Danger Response: How maladaptive and harmful signaling within the body causes complex chronic illnesses". The Forgotten Side of Medicine (Substack).

What Is Included vs. Excluded

  • Included (~6,200 words, seven public sections):
    1. Overview of the Cell Danger Response (CDR) and hypometabolic states
    2. The Healing Cycle (CDR1, CDR2, CDR3 and safety-signal termination)
    3. Rebooting The Healing Cycle (Naviaux's Six Cardinal Elements of the Health Cycle, salugenesis, vagal tone, sleep, rhythms, pulsed signaling)
    4. Blocking the CDR (extracellular ATP, purinergic receptors, experimental antipurinergic drugs, mast cell activation)
    5. Autism and Antipurinergic Therapy (mouse models, Naviaux's 2017 pilot trial, PaxMedica South Africa trial, suramin availability, ISR inhibition)
    6. Working with the CDR in Clinical Practice (clinician insights, sequencing, hormone resistance, fasting, ketogenic metabolism, induction therapy)
    7. Regenerative Medical Treatments of the CDR & the public opening of Systemic Regenerative Therapies (local scars, neural therapy notes, Alzheimer's dormancy framework, Telogen Effluvium analogy)
  • Excluded (paywalled): The subscriber-only second half of the essay covering specific systemic regenerative protocols. Readers who want that section can access it directly on Substack.

Attribution & Medical Disclaimer

The essay blends two distinct voices, kept separate throughout these pages:

  1. Dr. Robert K. Naviaux's framework & published research: Terminology such as the Cell Danger Response, Healing Cycle (CDR1–CDR3), Health Cycle, salugenesis, metabolomic/hypometabolic findings in ME/CFS and long-COVID, and low-dose suramin pilot research in autism spectrum disorder.
  2. A Midwestern Doctor's clinical commentary & hypotheses: Observations from integrative/regenerative practice, commentary on pharmaceuticals, vaccines, spike protein injuries, surgical anesthetics, reverse T3 / low-dose T3, zeta potential, DMSO, and institutional critiques.

These pages are study notes summarizing a public essay, not medical advice or peer-reviewed clinical guidelines.
