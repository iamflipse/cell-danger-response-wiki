Cell Danger Response & The Health Cycle

"The six cardinal elements of the health cycle are 1) wakeful activity, 2) nutrient intake, 3) waste and toxin removal, 4) social connection [and positive social support], 5) nature connection, and 6) restorative sleep." — Dr. Robert K. Naviaux, quoted in Systemic Treatments for the Cell Danger Response

Source Scope

  • Essay: A Midwestern Doctor (Jul 5, 2023). Systemic Treatments for the Cell Danger Response, The Forgotten Side of Medicine (Substack)
  • Scientific Foundation: Dr. Robert K. Naviaux's model of the Cell Danger Response (CDR), Healing Cycle, and Health Cycle (salugenesis)
  • What is covered here: The public first half (~6,200 words, seven sections). The subscriber-only second half covering specific systemic regenerative protocols is excluded; see About & Source Scope.

Core Thesis: Cultivating Health, Not Just Chasing Disease

Rather than treating chronic illness solely as isolated organ failures, the CDR framework reframes many complex, treatment-resistant conditions as cells trapped inside a protective defense loop that failed to receive the systemic safety signals needed to finish healing and rejoin the Health Cycle.
